# Quotes

Priced proposals, versioned. A quote is raised from a client's card; each revision is a new version, and a version that was sent never changes again.

## The list

Totals per state open the page, counted on the latest version of each quote. The table lists each quote with its version, the client, the state, the total, when it was sent and until when it is valid; an expired one says so. The filters take a search over the code, the client or the company, and a state: draft, sent, accepted, declined, expired or withdrawn.

## The quote's page

The tabs across the top are the versions. The lines of a draft version can be added, removed and reordered; those of a sent version cannot.

**Adding a line**

A line comes from the catalogue under Settings or carries a label of its own, with a quantity and a price. A catalogue line with a price range offers the low and the high end of the range; a catalogue entry with no price on the rate card in force is refused rather than guessed at, so put a price on the card or give it explicitly.

**Sending**

Arpenteur does not send a quote: you send it, from your own mail client, and Mark as sent records to whom. The version is then frozen. The print page renders the version for the browser's print dialogue, which is how a PDF is made.

**After sending**

- Accepted, Declined and Withdrawn record the client's answer, and the change is logged on the client's record.
- New version copies the latest lines into a fresh draft, to revise the offer.
- Raise an invoice, once accepted, opens a draft invoice copied line for line.

## The printed document

The print page carries your identity from Settings, the client's address, the offer's code and version, the validity date, the lines with their amounts, the net total, the VAT line or the sentence that applies when you are not subject to it, the total and your terms. What you see is what prints.
