# Calendar

The month: meetings, interviews, deadlines, and the start and end of each project. Nothing else.

## The grid

A month, Monday to Sunday, with up to four entries a day and a count for the rest. Each entry links to its client, or to its project for a start or a due date. The arrows move a month at a time; "today" returns to the current one. The list under the grid repeats what is still to come this month, with the client, the project and who it is with.

## The entries it holds

The calendar reads the same record as a client's timeline and shows a different cut of it:

- every entry still planned, whatever its kind, because something committed to on a stated date is what a diary is for
- meetings and deadlines after the fact, because "when did we meet them" is a question asked of a calendar
- the start and due date of every project, read from the project itself, so moving a deadline on the project moves the calendar

Everything else, an email filed, a document received, a status changed, a note typed after a call, stays on the client's timeline, where it can be read in order. A month grid carrying every update would bury the one meeting of the week under forty entries nobody has to attend.

## Adding an entry

There is no form here. Record a meeting or a deadline from the client's card, set to Planned, and it appears on its date. Marking it as happened or cancelled is done on the card too.
