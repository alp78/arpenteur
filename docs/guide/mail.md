# Mail

Correspondence read from your mailbox, matched to the company it came from or went to, and held here until you file it. Nothing reaches a client's timeline until you file it, and nothing in the mailbox is marked read, moved or deleted by this tool.

## The queue

Three tabs: waiting, filed and ignored, each with its count. A message shows whether it was sent or received, its subject and time, the addresses and the folder it was read from, the company proposed with the rule that matched it, and an excerpt. The text of a message is downloaded only when it matched a company, or when you file it by hand.

The rule that matched is named on each message: an address or a domain learned from an earlier filing, or an address held on the company. Mail from your own domains, listed under Settings, Mailbox, is never matched to a company: a website contact form sends from your own domain.

## Filing a message

- File, with a client chosen, writes the message to that client's timeline. A company that is not yet a client is named as a proposal and cannot be filed to until it is one.
- Ignore sets the message aside.
- Put back in the queue, on a filed or ignored message, undoes either.

Filing teaches the matcher: the address and domain are remembered for the company, and the company's page lists them under "Mail recognised by", where each can be forgotten.

## Where the messages come from

- The Read the mailbox card on Operations fetches envelopes read-only, over a window of days, from the folders named or from everything except the spam, trash, drafts and promotional folders. Free.
- The mailbox is named in the environment file, under the names the Mail section of Settings lists. Most Swiss hosts issue one password per application and refuse the account password over IMAP.

A second card, Read delivery failures, reads the same mailbox for bounces and refusals and records them on the addresses; it is what a campaign waits for between waves.
