# Audit of 13.10.2026: Arpenteur as a product

Three read-only audits ran on 13.10.2026 over the 40,580-line codebase: the console as a stranger meets it, the installation and the repository, the code's coherence and defects. Every item below was verified by reading the cited line or by running a read-only command. The verdicts on the items of `findings.md` (07.10.2026) are folded in here, so this file is the current register.

Severity: blocker means a stranger cannot use the feature or money and documents come out wrong; major means a feature is broken, unreachable or misleading; minor is polish. Each item names the stage of `todo.md` that fixes it.

Decisions taken by the operator on 13.10.2026:

- The git history stays as it is; the `_scratch` folder and the historical IBAN are cleaned up for the commercial version.
- The operator's own campaign material leaves git but stays on disk, and the repository ships neutral examples in its place.
- The licence is proprietary, all rights reserved.
- The six stages run in order, each ending green on main with its own commit.
